Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding smartphone screen repairs, battery health, charging ports, camera and audio checks, software data precautions, accessories, pricing estimates, and how online repair requests work.

Modern smartphones bond the outer protective glass, touch digitizer, and OLED or LCD panel together. Whether the outer glass is cracked or the display shows vertical lines, dark spots, or dead touch zones, our technician inspects the display and frame alignment to confirm the appropriate screen replacement assembly for your exact model.
No. Part availability and display specifications depend on your exact smartphone brand and model generation. We verify compatible screen options and share transparent pricing before any repair proceeds.
Rapid daily battery percentage drain, unexpected shutdowns while still showing 15%–35% charge, noticeable warmth during basic tasks, or physical swelling lifting the display or back cover.
Stop charging the device immediately, avoid pressing on the swollen screen or rear panel, and bring it in for safe professional inspection. Never attempt to pry out or puncture a lithium-ion battery at home.
The most frequent cause is compacted pocket lint sitting inside the USB-C or Lightning port, preventing the cable from seating fully—or worn/bent contact pins inside the charging port assembly. We inspect the port under magnification before recommending cleaning or replacement.
We strongly advise against inserting metal pins, needles, or sharp objects into a charging port, as you can easily bend delicate gold contact pins or short-circuit live power lines.
If the outer protective camera glass window is cracked but the camera still focuses clearly without dark sensor dust spots, replacing the exterior lens glass promptly can protect the internal camera module.
Smartphones use multiple microphones—a primary bottom microphone for standard handset calls and secondary microphones for speakerphone and noise cancellation. If the bottom microphone port is blocked or its flex circuit is damaged, handset voice calls will sound faint or silent.
Basic cache checks or settings reviews may not erase data, but operating system restores or factory resets permanently delete all user data on the device. We always remind customers to maintain an independent backup and never guarantee data recovery.
