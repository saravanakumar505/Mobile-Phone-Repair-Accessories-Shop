1. Website Usage & Independent Status

This website provides general information regarding FIXORA's mobile phone repair services and accessories catalogue. FIXORA operates as an independent repair and accessories shop and makes no claim of official manufacturer partnership or authorized service centre status.

2. Repair Enquiries vs. Confirmed Appointments

Submitting an online Repair Request prepares an enquiry for shop review. It does not constitute a binding repair contract, confirmed appointment slot, or guarantee that a specific replacement part is currently in stock.

3. Pricing Estimates & Device Inspection

All prices displayed on the website (including in the Pricing Guide and Accessories Catalogue) are illustrative, configurable examples. Final repair pricing is determined only after physical diagnostic inspection of the device and verification of model-specific part costs.

4. Repair Approval & Parts Availability

No repair work proceeds without customer approval of the post-inspection estimate. Component availability varies by smartphone brand, model generation, and supplier stock.
