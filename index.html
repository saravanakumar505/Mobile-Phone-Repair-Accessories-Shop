Symptom Guide

Common Signs Your Device Needs Inspection

Smartphone symptoms can often have multiple underlying causes—for example, a phone that will not charge might have a compacted port, a worn cable, a degraded battery, or a board-level fault. Proper diagnostic inspection identifies the real issue before you decide on a repair.

01 / Display

Cracked or Unresponsive Display

Surface glass fractures, touch dead zones, ghost touches, or dim backlight output requiring screen assembly inspection.

02 / Power

Rapid Battery Drain

Noticeably shorter battery runtime between charges or battery percentage dropping in sudden steps under load.

03 / Connector

Phone Not Charging

Intermittent charging connection, loose cable seat, or failure to negotiate standard or fast charging current.

04 / Thermal

Overheating

Unusual warmth during basic browsing or charging, which may relate to background software loops, battery wear, or moisture.

05 / Optics

Blurry Camera

Inability to lock autofocus, vibrating camera preview, dust behind cracked lens glass, or blank camera interface.

06 / Acoustics

No Sound From Speaker

Faint earpiece audio during calls or silent/crackling media playback from the bottom loudspeaker assembly.

07 / Voice Input

Microphone Problems

Muffled voice transmission on phone calls, silent voice notes, or static noise picked up by the microphone array.

08 / Stability

Unexpected Shutdown

Device turning off abruptly while still showing remaining battery charge, especially when launching camera or apps.

09 / System

Software Errors

Boot loops, stalled OS updates, full storage sluggishness, or system app crashes requiring software assessment.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs →

Does submitting the online repair form confirm an immediate appointment?

No. Submitting the form prepares a repair enquiry with your device details and symptoms. Our shop reviews part availability for your model and contacts you to confirm an inspection time and estimate.

How is the final repair price determined?

Prices listed on the website are illustrative starting examples. Final pricing depends on your exact phone model, part availability, and physical inspection findings—always shared for your approval before work starts.

Should I back up my phone data before bringing it in?

Yes. Whenever your device is functional enough to allow it, we strongly recommend backing up your photos, contacts, and files prior to any hardware or software inspection.

What should I do if my phone was exposed to water?

Power off the device immediately if safe to do so, do not plug in a charging cable, and bring it in for a water damage inspection as soon as possible to assess internal corrosion.
